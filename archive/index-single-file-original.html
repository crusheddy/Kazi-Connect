The problem

Hiring in Tanzania is broken in three ways

Both of our lines exist to answer the same three gaps.

01

No recruitment bandwidth

Growing companies can't scale hiring with a lean — or absent — HR team. Roles stay open for months while managers screen CVs between meetings.

02

No formal HR function

Mid-size firms run on informal contracts and paper records, leaving them exposed to labor-law risk they often discover only during a dispute.

03

Underserved options

Informal recruiters lack structure. Large agencies price out the mid-market. Generic HR software isn't built for how the Tanzanian market actually hires.

Why one company sells both

Kazi Connect was founded in Dar es Salaam by Emmanuel Enock Macha (Eddie), who brings an unusual pairing: a background in International Relations & Diplomacy, and hands-on software development — Eddie built the Kazi Connect HMS platform in-house.

That dual capability is exactly why the company has two lines. The relationships, negotiation, and institutional trust from the diplomacy side built the recruitment and HR services practice. Privacy, terms & data protection

Privacy Policy

What we collect. When you register as a candidate or send an enquiry we collect the information you provide: name, contact details, field of study or trade, location, sector interest, and availability. We do not buy or scrape personal data.

How we use it. Candidate data is used solely to match you with employment opportunities and to contact you about them. Enquiry data is used to respond to your request. We do not sell personal data to third parties. Candidate profiles are shared with a hiring client only when your profile is being considered for a specific role.

Retention and removal. You may request a copy, correction, or deletion of your data at any time by emailing emmanuelmacha123@gmail.com. Removal requests are actioned within 30 days.

Terms of Service

Services engagements are governed by a signed engagement letter that specifies scope, tier, SLAs, and monthly fees in Tanzanian Shillings. Either party may terminate with 30 days' written notice unless the engagement letter states otherwise.

HMS subscriptions are billed annually in US Dollars per the plan selected. Subscription fees are non-refundable once a billing year begins, except where required by law. Fair-use limits (job posts, team members) follow the published plan.

General. Content on this site is provided for information and does not constitute legal or financial advice. Case results describe specific past engagements and do not guarantee future outcomes. These terms are governed by the laws of the United Republic of Tanzania.

Data Protection — Tanzania PDPA (2022)

Kazi Connect processes personal data in accordance with Tanzania's Personal Data Protection Act, 2022 and its regulations. In practice this means: we collect data with your consent for a stated purpose; we use it only for that purpose; access inside our team is role-based; and you have the right to access, correct, or request erasure of your data.

Candidate records are stored in the Kazi Connect HMS platform with role-based access controls. HMS subscription customers are independent controllers of the candidate data they collect through their own hiring; the platform provides the access controls and deletion tooling to support their PDPA obligations.

To exercise any data right, email emmanuelmacha123@gmail.com with the subject "Data request".

Risks

Risks & mitigations, stated plainly

RiskMitigation
Founder dependency — delivery and product both trace to one person Phase 2 exists for this reason: documented playbooks, an operations lead hire, and HR Partners who own client relationships directly.
Client concentration in early cohorts Four-sector focus caps exposure; no single client above 15% of revenue as a standing policy from Year 2.
Receivables risk — NGO and corporate payment cycles run long Deposits on engagement, milestone billing, and 15% of the raise held as working capital.
Labor-law and regulatory change Compliance monitoring is a paid service we sell — the same watch we keep for Enterprise clients protects our own contracts.
SaaS execution risk — services firms often fail to become software companies HMS is not speculative: it already runs every services engagement. The raise funds packaging and go-to-market, not a first build.
Competition from regional job boards and global ATS products Local moat: verified candidate database, Tanzanian labor-law workflows, and TZS-market pricing that global products don't serve.
