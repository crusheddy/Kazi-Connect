About

Kazi Connect

A Dar es Salaam recruitment and HR company that runs its work on software it built itself - and sells that software to companies who hire in-house. New, deliberately focused, and straight about where it stands.

The company

What Kazi Connect is

Kazi Connect is a Dar es Salaam recruitment and HR company that operates its own hiring platform and sells access to that platform as a separate product.

The first line is Recruitment & HR Services. Our recruiters and HR Partners run hiring on a client's behalf: defining the role, sourcing and screening candidates, verifying credentials, coordinating interviews, and preparing compliant contracts. For companies with no formal HR function, an assigned HR Partner builds one. This line is priced in Tanzanian Shillings because it needs our people on the ground.

The second line is Kazi Connect HMS, the software those engagements run on, sold as a subscription to companies who hire in-house. It is priced in US Dollars because software crosses borders in a way a recruitment team does not.

We are deliberately focused: four sectors, two products, one market. We would rather be the obvious choice for a Tanzanian mid-market employer than a generic option for everyone.

The dual model

Why one company runs both

Recruitment companies don't usually build software, and software companies don't usually run recruitment. Kazi Connect does both - not as a hedge, but because each line makes the other stronger.

Services · Line 1

The services line

Done-for-you recruitment and HR operations. It is the line that puts us in the room with the banks, NGOs, manufacturers and universities whose hiring problems we exist to solve - and it is where our understanding of the market comes from. It is priced in Tanzanian Shillings and built for the Tanzanian mid-market.

Software · Line 2

The software line

The platform our services run on was built in-house rather than bought, because generic HR software assumes a hiring process that isn't ours. Selling it is the natural second line: tooling shaped by recruiters doing the work, rather than by guesswork about what recruiters might want.

The loop

Services work generates the market insight that shapes the software; the software makes the services work faster and more transparent than a spreadsheet-based competitor can manage. Neither line is a side project - the services line funds and proves the platform, and the platform is what lets the services line scale.

How we work

Four things we hold to

01

We screen before you see anyone

Forwarding CVs is not recruitment. Candidates are interviewed against an agreed scorecard and their credentials verified before they reach your shortlist - that is where the time saving actually comes from.

02

We report unrounded

If seven of eight roles fill, we will say seven of eight - not "almost all". A recruitment partner who smooths over the misses is not giving you information you can plan with. The same applies to this website: no invented case studies.

03

We say no to roles we'd handle badly

We focus on four sectors deliberately. If a role sits outside them and we don't think we'd do it justice, we'll tell you rather than take the retainer.

04

Candidate data is handled carefully

Candidates trust us with their information on the strength of a possible job. Consent is recorded at capture, access is role-based, and removal requests are honoured - see our data protection notice.

Leadership

Who runs it
