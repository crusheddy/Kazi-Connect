Why this matters

Software that was used before it was sold

Most hiring software is built by people who have never run a hiring campaign. HMS went the other way round. It was built in-house to solve our own recruiters' problems - tracking dozens of candidates across parallel roles, coordinating panels who never check email, proving to a client exactly where a role stands.

Every feature below exists because a Kazi Connect recruiter needed it to do their job. That is the bridge between our two business lines: the Services line proves the software daily, and the software line lets companies who don't need our recruiters buy the tooling anyway. When we ship a change, our own team feels it first.

Data & security

How candidate data is handled

Your data stays yours

Candidates you collect in your HMS account are yours. We don't move them into the Kazi Connect talent pool, and our recruiters don't search them. The verified candidate database is a Services asset and is kept separate.

Built around the PDPA (2022)

Access is role-based, consent is recorded at the point of capture, and deletion tooling exists so you can honour erasure requests. As an HMS subscriber you are the controller of your candidate data; we provide the controls. See our data protection notice.

Common questions

Before you subscribe

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Get in touch through the contact page and we'll set up a trial account so you can run a real role through it before committing to an annual plan.

Why is the software priced in USD when you're a Tanzanian company?

Because the software sells across borders - a hiring team in Nairobi or Kampala can subscribe as easily as one in Dar es Salaam. Our Services line, which requires our people on the ground, is priced in Tanzanian Shillings. We never mix the two.

Do we get access to your candidate database with a subscription?

No - and we're explicit about this. The verified candidate database is part of the Services line, where our recruiters search it on your behalf. An HMS subscription gives you the tooling to manage your own candidates.

What happens when we outgrow the Growth plan?

Scale removes the job post and team member limits and adds SSO, custom integrations, a dedicated account manager, and an SLA. It's priced per agreement because the requirements vary considerably at that size.

Can we switch to done-for-you Services if we can't fill a role?

Yes. Some subscribers run most hiring themselves and bring us in for hard-to-fill or senior roles. Because both lines run on HMS, there's no migration involved - see Services.

Is the subscription refundable?

Annual subscription fees are non-refundable once a billing year begins, except where required by law - which is exactly why we'd rather you ran a trial first. Full terms are on the Terms of Service page.
