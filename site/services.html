Our process

Five stages, every engagement

The same sequence applies to every role, whether it's one hire or a multi-role campaign. Each stage has a defined output, so you always know where things stand.

  1. 1

    Role & Requirements Definition

    We sit down with the hiring manager - not just HR - to establish what the role actually needs to deliver in its first year. We separate genuine requirements from wish-list items, agree the compensation band against what the market is currently paying, and write the scorecard the shortlist will be judged against.

    Role scorecard Agreed compensation band Signed-off job description
  2. 2

    Sourcing Strategy

    Every role gets a channel plan rather than a single job advert. That usually combines a search of our verified candidate database, targeted outreach to people already doing the job elsewhere, and - for entry-level and technical trades - our university, college and VETA partnerships. Professional and Enterprise tiers include direct database search.

    Channel plan Published job post Longlist
  3. 3

    Screening & Verification

    We interview candidates ourselves against the scorecard before you see anyone - no CV forwarding. Credentials are verified with the issuing institution and references are taken up directly. Candidates who don't hold up at this stage never reach your desk, which is where most of the time saving comes from.

    Structured interview notes Verified credentials Reference checks
  4. 4

    Shortlist & Interview Coordination

    You receive a ranked shortlist with the reasoning behind each ranking, not an undifferentiated pile. We then handle the scheduling - panels, candidates, reschedules - and collect structured feedback from every interviewer so the decision is made on comparable evidence rather than whoever spoke last.

    Ranked shortlist Scheduled interviews Panel feedback summary
  5. 5

    Offer, Contract & Onboarding

    We support the offer conversation, including counter-offer situations, and prepare a contract that complies with Tanzanian labor law. Onboarding doesn't stop at day one - we stay involved through the probation period, which is where placements most often fail if nobody is watching.

    Offer negotiation support Compliant contract Probation check-ins

Engagement questions

How working with us actually works

Is this a retainer or per-placement commission?

A monthly retainer. You pay the tier fee and we work the roles in scope - there is no separate percentage-of-salary commission per hire. This is deliberate: it keeps our incentive on the right hire rather than the most expensive one.

How many roles can we run at once?

Basic is designed around occasional, single-role hiring. Professional supports structured multi-role campaigns running in parallel. Enterprise has no fixed role cap because it's a full HR outsourcing arrangement - the scope is agreed in the engagement letter.

What does the assigned HR Partner on Enterprise actually do?

They function as your HR department. That means owning your employment contracts, writing and maintaining the staff handbook, converting paper files into digital HR records, advising on disciplinary and grievance processes, giving payroll guidance, and monitoring changes in labor law that affect you. Companies typically compare this against the cost of employing an internal HR Director.

Do we get access to your candidate database?

Direct database search is included on Professional and Enterprise. On Basic, our recruiters search the database on your behalf. Note that database access is a Services benefit - it is not part of the HMS software subscription, which is for managing your own candidates.

Can we start with Services and move to the software later?

Yes, and some clients do exactly that - they use Services while they build an internal hiring capability, then move to an HMS subscription once they have a team to run it. Because the work already happens on HMS, the history comes with you.

How do we end the engagement?

30 days' written notice, unless your engagement letter specifies otherwise. Full terms are on the Terms of Service page.
