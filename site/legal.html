Legal & policies

Plain-language versions of how we handle your data, what our engagements commit each side to, and the standards we hold ourselves and our clients to. Nothing here is legal advice; for an engagement, the signed engagement letter governs.

01

Privacy Policy

What we collect

When you register as a candidate or send an enquiry we collect the information you provide: name, contact details, field of study or trade, location, sector interest, and availability. For employer enquiries we collect your name, email, organization, and the details of your message. We do not buy or scrape personal data.

How we use it

Candidate data is used solely to match you with employment opportunities and to contact you about them. Enquiry data is used to respond to your request. We do not sell personal data to third parties. A candidate profile is shared with a hiring client only when that candidate is being considered for a specific role.

Retention and removal

You may request a copy, correction, or deletion of your data at any time by emailing emmanuelmacha123@gmail.com. Removal requests are actioned within 30 days.

This website

This site sets no tracking cookies and runs no third-party analytics. The only external resource loaded is a charting library used on the investor page. Form submissions are sent to our own endpoint; theme preference is held in memory for the current page view only and is not stored on your device.

03

Data Protection - Tanzania PDPA (2022)

Kazi Connect processes personal data in accordance with Tanzania's Personal Data Protection Act, 2022 and its regulations. In practice that means we collect data with your consent for a stated purpose, use it only for that purpose, restrict access inside our team by role, and honour your right to access, correct, or erase your data.

Your rights

  • Ask what personal data we hold about you
  • Have inaccurate data corrected
  • Withdraw consent and have your data erased
  • Object to your profile being shared with a particular employer

Candidates in the talent pool

Candidate records are stored in the Kazi Connect HMS platform with role-based access controls. Consent is recorded at the point of registration. Your profile is shared with a client only when you are being considered for a specific role.

HMS subscription customers

Companies subscribing to the HMS software are independent controllers of the candidate data they collect through their own hiring. Kazi Connect provides the access controls, consent capture, and deletion tooling to support their obligations under the Act; the subscriber remains responsible for how they use them.

Making a request

Email emmanuelmacha123@gmail.com with the subject "Data request". We action requests within 30 days.

Note: we describe our practice under Tanzanian law. We do not claim compliance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation.

04

Cancellation & Refund Policy

Services engagements Line 1

Recruitment and HR services are billed monthly in Tanzanian Shillings. Either party may cancel with 30 days' written notice, unless your engagement letter sets a different period. The current month's retainer is payable in full; we do not pro-rate a month that has already begun, because the sourcing and screening work for that month is already underway.

If we have not delivered the work agreed for a paid month - no shortlists produced on an active role, for example - raise it with us. Where the shortfall is ours, we will credit the following month rather than argue about it.

HMS subscriptions Line 2

Subscriptions are billed annually in US Dollars. Annual fees are non-refundable once a billing year begins, except where Tanzanian law requires otherwise. This is why we offer a free trial: run a live role through the platform before you commit to a year.

You may cancel at any time to prevent the next annual renewal. Cancelling stops the renewal; it does not refund the current year. Your account stays active until the end of the paid period, and you can export your candidate data before it closes.

Downgrades and upgrades

Upgrading mid-year is charged pro-rata for the remainder of the billing year. Downgrades take effect at the next renewal, not immediately.

How to cancel

Email emmanuelmacha123@gmail.com with the subject "Cancellation". We acknowledge within two business days and confirm the effective date in writing.

05

Code of Conduct

This code applies to our own team, to candidates in our talent pool, and to clients who engage us or subscribe to the platform. It exists because recruitment gives us influence over people's livelihoods, and that has to be handled carefully.

What we commit to

  • We assess candidates against the agreed role scorecard - not on ethnicity, religion, gender, disability, marital status, or political affiliation.
  • We never charge candidates a fee. Registration and placement are free for candidates; our clients pay us. Anyone asking a candidate for money in our name is not acting for Kazi Connect.
  • We tell candidates the truth about a role - including salary band, contract type, and duration - before they interview.
  • We share a candidate's profile with a client only for a specific role under consideration.
  • We report outcomes unrounded, including the roles we failed to fill.

What we ask of candidates

  • Give accurate information about your qualifications and experience. We verify credentials with issuing institutions, and misrepresentation ends the application.
  • Attend interviews you accept, or tell us in advance if you cannot.
  • Tell us if you withdraw or accept another offer, so we can be straight with the client.

What we ask of clients

  • Advertise and honour the compensation actually agreed for the role.
  • Employ people on terms compliant with Tanzanian labor law.
  • Use candidate data only to assess the role it was shared for.
  • Give interview feedback within a reasonable time, so candidates are not left waiting.

Raising a concern

If anyone acting for Kazi Connect breaches this code - including a demand for payment from a candidate - email emmanuelmacha123@gmail.com with the subject "Conduct". Reports are reviewed by the Managing Director. We may end an engagement, or remove an account, where a client or candidate breaches this code.

Our content

The text, design, and the Kazi Connect name and logo on this site are owned by Kazi Connect. You may quote or link to our material with attribution. You may not republish substantial parts of it as your own, or present our published material as your own results.

Your content

Job posts, scorecards, interview notes, and candidate records you create in Kazi Connect HMS remain your property. We claim no ownership of them. We process that content only to provide the service, and we do not move your candidates into our talent pool or make them searchable by our recruiters.

Acceptable use of the platform

Subscribers agree not to:

  • Post fraudulent vacancies, or roles that do not exist.
  • Use the platform to collect personal data for a purpose other than hiring for the advertised role.
  • Charge applicants a fee to apply for a role.
  • Upload candidate data obtained without the candidate's consent.
  • Share account credentials outside the licensed team-member count, or attempt to access another organization's data.
  • Scrape, resell, or redistribute the platform or its data.

We may suspend an account for a breach of these rules, and will do so immediately where candidate safety or data is at risk.

Reporting infringement or misuse

To report a copyright infringement or a fraudulent job post, email emmanuelmacha123@gmail.com with the subject "Infringement" or "Report a post", identifying the material and the basis of your complaint. We respond within five business days.
