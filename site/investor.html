Investor briefing · Confidential

Services pay for today. The platform is the upside.

Kazi Connect's dual model is the investment thesis. The recruitment and HR services line is the near-term revenue engine - cash-generative from the first engagements, and the reason the platform gets built against real delivery rather than guesswork. Kazi Connect HMS is the scale and margin story: software that sells cross-border in USD, on top of a services business designed to pay its own way.

Current status

Kazi Connect is pre-revenue and has not yet delivered client engagements. There is no trading history behind the figures on this page - every number here is a forward projection. What exists today is the HMS platform (built and working), a recruitment and HR Partner team, university/college/VETA sourcing partnerships, and a verified candidate database. What does not yet exist is a client book. We would rather you priced that risk accurately than discovered it later.

Risks

Risks & mitigations, stated plainly

Risk Mitigation
No trading history - pre-revenue, no clients delivered to date This is the primary risk and we do not dress it up. Mitigation is that the capital-intensive parts are already done: the platform is built, the team is hired, sourcing partnerships are signed and candidates are registered. The unproven step is client acquisition, not build-out.
Founder dependency - delivery and product both trace to one person Phase 2 exists for this reason: documented playbooks, an operations lead hire, and HR Partners who own client relationships directly.
Client concentration in early cohorts Four-sector focus caps exposure; no single client above 15% of revenue as a standing policy from Year 2.
Receivables risk - NGO and corporate payment cycles run long Deposits on engagement, milestone billing, and 15% of the raise held as working capital.
Labor-law and regulatory change Compliance monitoring is a paid service we sell - the same watch we keep for Enterprise clients protects our own contracts.
SaaS execution risk - services firms often fail to become software companies HMS is not speculative: it already runs every services engagement. The raise funds packaging and go-to-market, not a first build.
Competition from regional job boards and global ATS products Local moat: verified candidate database, Tanzanian labor-law workflows, and TZS-market pricing that global products don't serve.
