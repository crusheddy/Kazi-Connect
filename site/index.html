The problem

Hiring in Tanzania is broken in three ways

Both of our lines exist to answer the same three gaps.

01

No recruitment bandwidth

Growing companies can't scale hiring with a lean - or absent - HR team. Roles stay open for months while managers screen CVs between meetings.

02

No formal HR function

Mid-size firms run on informal contracts and paper records, leaving them exposed to labor-law risk they often discover only during a dispute.

03

Underserved options

Informal recruiters lack structure. Large agencies price out the mid-market. Generic HR software isn't built for how the Tanzanian market actually hires.
